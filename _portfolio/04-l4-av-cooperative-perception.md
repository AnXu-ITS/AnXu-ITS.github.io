## 📌 Project Overview

* **Host Institution**: **IABG mbH** (Mobility Innovation Campus, Munich, Germany)
* **Scope**: L4 Autonomous Shuttle / Vehicle Testing, Cooperative Intelligent Transportation Systems (C-ITS).

---

## 🚗 Core Systems & Engineering Work

1. **BEV Sensor Fusion**:
   * Fused onboard Camera, LiDAR, Radar, and infrastructure RSU streams into unified Bird's-Eye-View (BEV) scene representations.
   * Enhanced perception coverage in occluded intersection and blind-spot scenarios.

2. **V2X Infrastructure Integration**:
   * Developed Python middleware handling real-time SPaT (Signal Phase and Timing) and MAPEM broadcast decoding.
   * Integrated roadside perception units with vehicle trajectory planning algorithms.

3. **Scenario Testing & Trajectory Analysis**:
   * Executed real-world validation test runs at the MIC testbed.
   * Automated telemetry data analysis, safety metric logging (TTC, PET), and scenario corner case extraction.
