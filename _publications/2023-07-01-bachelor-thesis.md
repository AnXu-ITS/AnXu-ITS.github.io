## Summary

This experimental thesis analyzed the physical and chemical property modifications in asphalt binders induced by Sasobit wax additives. Through laboratory rheological and penetration tests, the study evaluated temperature reduction benefits and mechanical performance improvements for sustainable highway engineering applications.
