## Summary

This paper investigates the transformative impact of artificial intelligence technologies across four key pillars of modern transportation: Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS), Autonomous Vehicles, Smart Railways, and Urban Traffic Management. It contrasts traditional heuristic control approaches with modern data-driven and LLM-agent-based paradigms, outlining future research roadmaps for resilient, closed-loop urban mobility.
